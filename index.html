Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

What are your delivery times?+
We offer standard delivery (3–5 business days) and express delivery (1–2 business days). Orders placed before 2pm on weekdays are dispatched the same day. Free delivery on all orders over $50.
Are all Fusion Beauty products cruelty-free?+
Absolutely. We are proudly 100% cruelty-free and never test on animals at any stage of production. Our entire range is certified and we only partner with suppliers who share our ethical values.
What is your return and exchange policy?+
We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unopened, unused products in their original packaging. Simply contact our customer care team and we'll arrange a hassle-free return or exchange for you.
How do I find my perfect foundation shade?+
Start by identifying your undertone — warm, cool, or neutral. Our AI Beauty Assistant can help narrow down your shade based on your skin tone. You can also request a free sample kit from our customer care team before committing to a full size.
Do you offer samples or travel sizes?+
Yes! Join our Beauty Club and receive a complimentary sample kit with your first order. We also offer travel-size versions of our bestselling serums and moisturisers — perfect for on-the-go glam.
